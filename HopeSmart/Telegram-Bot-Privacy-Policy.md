# HopeSmart International Settlement Agency Inc.
## Telegram Bot Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 9, 2026  
**Applies to:** HopeSmart's Telegram bot and related Telegram client-support communications  
**Organization:** HopeSmart International Settlement Agency Inc., Toronto, Ontario, Canada  
**Privacy contact:** [hopesmartisa@gmail.com](mailto:hopesmartisa@gmail.com)

### 1. Who we are and what this policy covers
HopeSmart International Settlement Agency Inc. ("HopeSmart," "we," "us") provides general newcomer settlement support, pre-arrival information, education and application assistance, transportation and airport pickup coordination, tours, consultations, and information about the Global African Conference. This policy describes how we handle personal information when someone contacts or interacts with our HopeSmart Telegram bot. It does not replace Telegram's own privacy policy or any separate privacy notice that applies when a client uses another service.

HopeSmart is an independent business. Our Telegram bot is not operated by Telegram, the Government of Canada, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), or any provincial government. The bot does not provide regulated immigration or legal representation.

### 2. Information that may be collected
When you interact with our bot, Telegram may make available your Telegram user ID, display name, username, profile information and selected language, along with the messages, button selections, files, and message timestamps you send or generate while interacting with the bot.

If you choose to provide them, we may also receive your name, preferred contact method, telephone number, email address, language preference, service interests, appointment requests, and limited information needed to understand a service inquiry (for example, whether you are seeking pre-arrival orientation, settlement support, education assistance, or airport pickup information).

**We do not automatically receive your telephone number or your unrelated private Telegram conversations simply because you open the bot.** Do not send Social Insurance Numbers, passwords, payment-card numbers, passport copies, immigration case files, medical information, children's identifying documents, or other sensitive records through the bot. We may direct you to a more appropriate, secure process if documents are necessary.

### 3. Purposes for using information
We use information received through the bot, as appropriate, to:
- Answer questions and explain HopeSmart services, eligibility limits, general processes and available next steps;
- Respond to pre-arrival, settlement, education, transportation, tour and conference-related inquiries;
- Arrange requested consultations or staff follow-up;
- Identify your preferred language, including English or Amharic;
- Maintain service communications and reasonable operational records;
- Detect misuse, protect the service, resolve complaints and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We limit collection and use to purposes that are appropriate and explained to you. We do not sell personal information.

### 4. Automated replies and artificial intelligence
The bot may use menus, automated messages or, when enabled, AI-assisted responses. We will identify automated interactions as such. If an AI or automation service is connected, information you submit to the bot may be processed by that service to produce or route a response, subject to the provider's applicable terms and privacy practices. We will assess providers and update this notice as appropriate before material changes in processing.

Automated messages are general service information, not personalized legal, immigration, medical or financial advice. A human representative can be requested for questions that require individual review. We do not promise immigration decisions, benefits, school admission, visas, or other outcomes through the bot.

### 5. When information may be shared
Information may be accessible to authorized HopeSmart personnel and to service providers used to deliver messaging, customer support, hosting or automation, to the extent necessary for their tasks. Telegram operates the Telegram messaging platform independently and processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

We may also disclose information to external professionals or referral organizations when you ask us to arrange a referral and we have the appropriate consent or other lawful authority. Information may be disclosed where required or permitted by applicable law, including to respond to a valid legal process.

We do not use information submitted for a service inquiry to send unrelated promotional messages without any consent required by applicable law.

### 6. International processing
Telegram and any future technology providers may process or store information outside Ontario or Canada. Foreign laws may apply to information processed in another country, and lawful access by authorities in that jurisdiction may be possible. Ask us about the providers used for your inquiry before sending sensitive details.

### 7. Consent and your choices
Where applicable law requires consent, we seek meaningful consent for the collection, use and disclosure of personal information. You can choose not to provide optional information; some requested services may then be unavailable. You may stop messaging the bot at any time. You may withdraw consent, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice, by contacting us.

Where we send commercial electronic messages, we will comply with applicable consent, identification and unsubscribe requirements. Service communications are not automatically permission for unrelated marketing.

### 8. Retention and deletion
We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purposes and applicable legal or business recordkeeping requirements, after which it should be securely deleted or anonymized. Actual retention may vary depending on the nature of the inquiry, whether it becomes an active client file, and provider retention settings. Deleting a conversation from your own Telegram account does not necessarily delete copies previously received by HopeSmart, Telegram, or a service provider. Contact us to request deletion from records under HopeSmart's control, subject to legal exceptions.

### 9. Safeguards
We aim to use reasonable access controls, confidentiality practices, minimization, staff access limits and appropriate provider arrangements. However, no online messaging service is risk-free. Standard Telegram bot conversations are not Telegram Secret Chats; do not assume bot conversations have end-to-end encryption. Please use an approved secure document-submission channel if we need sensitive records.

### 10. Children and family information
Parents or guardians should avoid sending sensitive information about children through the bot. Where a service involves a child, we will seek any consent required by applicable law and limit collection to what is necessary for the service.

### 11. Your privacy rights and complaints
Subject to applicable law, you may ask what personal information we hold about you, request access or correction, withdraw consent where applicable, or request deletion. To make a request or raise a concern, contact:

**HopeSmart International Settlement Agency Inc. — Privacy Contact**  
**Email:** hopesmartisa@gmail.com  
**Location:** Toronto, Ontario, Canada

We may need to verify your identity before releasing personal information. We will respond in accordance with applicable privacy law. You can also learn about privacy rights and complaints at the [Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/).

### 12. Third-party links and changes
If the bot links to a third-party site, that site's separate privacy practices apply. We may revise this policy as bot functions and service providers change. The latest public version will display its effective date. If a change materially affects how we handle personal information, we will provide any notice or choice required by law.
